# Minecraft agents: what prior work means for gtnh-agent

Research note for the gtnh-agent team, 2026-09-30. Sources are cited inline. Claims marked _(unverified)_ rest on secondary sources only or could not be confirmed in a primary source. Numbers attributed to "our estimate" are our own arithmetic or simulation, not published results.

## 1. TL;DR

- **"Bots speedrun Minecraft now" is only partly true.** The viral 8:43 Ender Dragon kill (GPT-6 Astra planner + TypeSafe's JEV chooser, Sept 2026) ran on a fixed, well-known seed, on Peaceful, with End portal coordinates supplied, a route surveyed in a separate test world, and full visibility of loaded chunks ([README](https://github.com/rmalde/minecraft-agent)). It is a route replay, not a random-seed speedrun. The fastest documented _random-seed_ autonomous run is still a hand-scripted bot (AltoClef on Baritone), at "under one hour" (Dec 2023, self-reported on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l7LzfHnyiUM)). That is about 8x the human random-seed record of 6:39 ([speedrun.com](https://www.speedrun.com/api/v1/runs/y44238ky)). No learned (RL) policy has finished the game.
- **Every agent that went deep relies on a strong scripted action layer.** Planning is rarely the bottleneck. DEPS goes from 0.59% to 60% diamond success when its learned controller is replaced by an oracle ([DEPS](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2302.01560)). That backs gtnh-agent's typed-skill design.
- **Pixel control is out of reach on a laptop.** VPT used 720 V100s for 9 days to get a 2.5% diamond-pickaxe rate ([VPT](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2206.11795)). Dreamer 4 used 256–1024 TPU-v5p for 0.7% diamond ([Dreamer 4](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.24527)).
- **Most headline results get help for free:** x-ray block search, op commands, Peaceful, permanent daylight, keepInventory, faster block breaking, fixed seeds. gtnh-agent's client receives full chunk data, so it already has x-ray unless we filter it.
- **No published agent plays GTNH or any 1.7.10 pack.** The nearest are two weeks-old hobby projects with no measured results. Age 0 would be a new benchmark.
- **A protocol-only client can complete and verify all 92 Age 0 quests.** Better Questing 3.7.15-GTNH exposes quest state and accepts checkbox, submit, choice and claim as plain packets ([BQ source](https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/BetterQuesting/tree/3.7.15-GTNH)). Every Age 0 mod block can be operated with vanilla packets plus one Tinkers' Construct payload. Both need new client work before BQ records anything past the first quest.
- **Recipe knowledge belongs in data, not in the 14B model.** Exact recipe lookup lifted Llama-70B from 0.26 to 0.67 on Plancraft ([Plancraft](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.21033v2)). XENON shows a 7B planner on top of an algorithmically corrected dependency graph beating larger proprietary models ([XENON](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.24157)).
- **qwen3:14b is a reasonable planner choice, but not a real-time one.** Expect roughly 10–15 s per non-thinking plan on this GPU, and about a minute with thinking (our estimate). Zero-shot qwen2.5:0.5b is not a reliable router.

## 2. The landscape by family

**Low-level learned control (pixels, keyboard and mouse).** VPT, the MineRL competitions, DreamerV3, Dreamer 4, STEVE-1, GROOT, ROCKET, JARVIS-VLA, Optimus-3, CrossHA, Game-TARS and SIMA. These systems match the human interface, but they are expensive and unreliable past the iron tier. DreamerV3, the first agent to find diamonds without human data, finds one in only 0.4% of episodes at its 100M-step budget. It also uses abstract crafting, an inventory vector and block breaking at 100x speed ([DreamerV3 v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.04104)). Two caveats: Dreamer 4's project page says it uses "100x less data" than VPT, but compared with the ~70k hours VPT actually trained on the gap is about 28x ([Dreamer 4 page](https://danijar.com/project/dreamer4/)). In the 2021 MineRL research track the winner (X3) reached iron ingot, not the iron pickaxe, in the official evaluation ([MineRL 2021](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2202.10583)).

**LLM agents over skill APIs.** Voyager, GITM, DEPS, Plan4MC, JARVIS-1, ODYSSEY, MP5, ADAM, XENON, Mindcraft, Project Sid and VillagerAgent. Most sit on Mineflayer. Voyager's GPT-4 writes JavaScript, and the environment quietly sets keepInventory, stops the daylight cycle, teleports the bot when it is stuck and gives back crafting tables after each step ([Voyager env](https://github.com/MineDojo/Voyager/blob/main/voyager/env/mineflayer/index.js)). Its README tells users to create a Creative, Peaceful world ([README](https://github.com/MineDojo/Voyager)). GITM's hand-written structured actions reached 67.5% diamond success with gpt-3.5 and say they avoid x-ray. They still rely on LiDAR rays, a 10-unit voxel radius used for navigation, and break speed 100 ([GITM](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.17144v2.pdf)).

**Scripted bots and full-game runs.** Baritone (A* pathing), AltoClef (a task tree plus survival "chains" that bid priorities), Mineflayer and its plugins, the Astra+JEV 8:43 run, and a Claude Opus 5 agent that beat Minecraft 1.21.4 in 3 h 56 min through 37 MCP tools ([dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/aibengineering/beat-the-game-minecraft)). Frontier LLMs driving the screen alone do poorly. On Vals AI's CUA-bench, "like every model", Claude Opus 5 never found iron in three hours ([CUA-bench](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/cua_bench)). One research angle reported mean scores of 10–13/100; another found the extracted numbers ambiguous, so treat those scores as _(unverified)_.

**Modded and tech-tree work.** MineMind ([repo](https://github.com/Boyan253/minemind); Forge 1.20.1, Reclamation pack) uses a privileged server-side entity that crafts through the server's RecipeManager. Its fork CoopBot ([repo](https://github.com/deviant46n2/minemind)) uses a local Qwen3-14B and an FTB Quests DAG, needs op to mark quests complete, and hands crafting to a human. Neither reports completion numbers. The Factorio Learning Environment is the closest analogue for industrial progression: Claude 3.5 Sonnet solved 7 of 24 lab-play tasks, and models could not coordinate more than about six machines ([FLE](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.09617)). Our critic pass also flagged Polycraft World (a Forge-mod tech-tree testbed from DARPA SAIL-ON) as prior art; it is _(unverified here)_.

## 3. Modded Minecraft and GTNH

**Prior work.** None at 1.7.10 or GregTech. MineMind and CoopBot share our goal source (a quest book) and, in CoopBot's case, our planner model. Both show the shortcuts we avoid: server-side crafting and op-level quest completion.

**Data sources we can use.**

- _Recipes._ The NESQL exporter dumps crafting, furnace, GT5 recipe maps, ore dictionary, fluids and BQ quests into one database. For GTNH that is about 600 MB and about 60 min ([NESQL](https://github.com/D-Cysteine/nesql-exporter)). Upstream was last pushed in 2024; the ShadowTheAge fork was pushed in 2026. RecEx writes JSON for GT5, Bartworks and GT++, and is kept under the GTNH org ([RecEx](https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/RecEx)). The GTNH Factory Flow pipeline exports per-release JSON from a headless client in CI, but its datasets are not in the repo ([Factory Flow](https://github.com/jackwrichards/gtnh-factory-flow)). None of these documents Tinkers' Construct coverage, and none states it runs on 2.8.4. The few Age 0 Tinkers recipes may be cheaper to hand-code.
- _Item catalog._ gtnh-craft-monitor-data has 10,984 catalog IDs and 52,449 icons for 2.8.4 ([release](https://github.com/Maselkov/gtnh-craft-monitor-data/releases/tag/gtnh-data-2.8.4)).
- _Quests._ The repo already parses DefaultQuests (`scripts/extract-quests.ts`). The server's database, read from `quest_sync`, is authoritative. BQ only loads DefaultQuests into a world that has no QuestDatabase.json.
- _Strategy text._ The GTNH wiki is CC BY-SA 4.0, but parts are stale for 2.8.4. The Coke Oven page describes GT's newer hatch oven, not the Railcraft oven 2.8.4 ships, and the ore-grid formula is the legacy one ([GT commit](https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/GT5-Unofficial/commit/52d79363506e2e0eb732c1103cecc7313de3fd00)). Pin mechanics to source tags, not to the wiki.

**1.7.10 tooling.** Mineflayer and prismarine-chunk start at 1.8. node-minecraft-protocol frames 1.7.10, and minecraft-protocol-forge does the FML handshake, but neither decodes NotEnoughIDs chunk formats ([nmp](https://github.com/PrismarineJS/node-minecraft-protocol)). The custom client was the right call. HeadlessMC plus hmc-specifics lists 1.7.10 Forge support ([hmc-specifics](https://headlesshq.github.io/headlessmc/specifics/)); it is useful as a byte-capture oracle. Whether it runs the GTNH stack is untested.

**What the source audits found for Age 0.**

- _Better Questing._ Channel `BQ_NET_CHAN`, FML discriminator 0. The payload is an NBT container of gzip(NBT) slices of at most 20,480 bytes, and a String `ID` picks the handler ([PacketAssembly](https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/BetterQuesting/blob/524b365211b6b3a9672cab8ae45b4e07e726d49e/src/main/java/betterquesting/network/PacketAssembly.java#L35-L113)). The server pushes the quest database, this player's progress and the active-quest set. Checkbox, submit (`quest_action` 1), choice and claim (`quest_action` 0) are plain packets with no GUI, distance or rate check. Quests complete in a 3 s server loop, and only while they are in `cache_sync.activeQuests`. Crafts, kills and inventory scans count only for active quests. 12 of the 35 crafting tasks ignore crafts made before their quest unlocks. Every reward needs a manual claim.
- _The chapter is not self-contained._ All 92 quests also require 14 outside quests: the 10-quest "And So It Begins" chain (including a consume task and a checkbox); a Tinkers smeltery, through one of two mutually exclusive (XOR) quests; and a LootGames dungeon block. `src/goals/quest-goals.ts` treats outside prerequisites as already met, ignores taskLogic OR, and counts a crafting task done when the item is held. With today's client, BQ would record 0 of the 92.
- _Mod blocks._ Every Age 0 interaction reduces to vanilla packets (C08, C0E with S32, C07, C0B, C05/C06), plus one required Tinkers payload: PatternTablePacket on channel `TConstruct` for stencil choice. Its discriminator, 0x08, is derived from a sort rule and has not been captured ([PacketPipeline](https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/TinkersConstruct/blob/1.13.57-GTNH/src/main/java/tconstruct/util/network/PacketPipeline.java#L143-L157)). No Age 0 GUI uses ModularUI. ModularUI 1.2.20 does add a VarInt stack size after every item stack's NBT, inside mod payloads too ([mixin](https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/ModularUI/blob/1.2.20/src/main/java/com/gtnewhorizons/modularui/mixins/early/PacketBufferMixin.java#L26-L48)). The smeltery GUI appears never to send slot updates, so its state must be read from the controller's tile data. The coke oven is 26 Railcraft blocks in a hollow 3x3x3, with no other oven block in the surrounding 5x5x5. GT removes log-to-charcoal furnace recipes, so the coke oven is the charcoal source.

## 4. What worked and what did not

**Worked.**

- _Hierarchy, with the investment in the executor._ GITM, ODYSSEY and the Opus 5 MCP run all win through robust scripted skills. JARVIS-1 names the controller as its bottleneck ([JARVIS-1](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.05997v3)).
- _Verification against game state._ Removing Voyager's self-verification cut discovered items by 73% ([Voyager](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2305.16291)). Project Sid credits an expected-versus-observed module ([PIANO](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.00114)).
- _Deterministic reflexes outside the LLM._ AltoClef's chains bid numeric priorities each tick: lava or fire 100, falling 100, flee unbeatable mobs 80, danger 70, food 55, main task 50 ([TaskRunner](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gaucho-matrero/altoclef/main/src/main/java/adris/altoclef/tasksystem/TaskRunner.java)). Mindcraft's modes interrupt any action ([modes.js](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mindcraft-bots/mindcraft/develop/src/agent/modes.js)).
- _Memory of plans, not code._ GITM summarizes five successful action sequences into one reusable plan. JARVIS-1 retrieves plans by situation.
- _Sparse reasoning._ Game-TARS scored 63% thinking only at key moments, against 45% thinking every step ([Game-TARS](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.23691)).

**Did not work.**

- _Small models writing code._ Voyager with 7–8B open models collected dirt 4–27% of the time and never made wooden tools ([MindForge](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.12977v3)). In Orak every open-source model scored 0 on Minecraft ([Orak](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.03610v3)).
- _Trusting LLM recipe knowledge._ Plan4MC hand-fixed 6 of 55 LLM-generated skills ([Plan4MC](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2303.16563)). XENON found that a 7B repeats the same errors despite failure feedback.
- _Recovering after catastrophic loss._ In the 141-hour Astra run, a creeper destroyed the chest and bed, and the agent spent hours farming potatoes _(secondary: [Gigazine](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260917-gpt-6-astra-plays-minecraft/))_.
- _Deep prerequisite chains._ On MineExplorer the best model fell from about 78% at one hop to 12.3% at four ([MineExplorer](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.30931v4)).
- _LLM spatial output._ GPT-4o failed almost all TeamCraft coordinate tasks, and MineAnyBuild tops out at 41/100.
- _Long-tail stuck states in scripted bots:_ soul sand, oceans, terrain edge cases.

**Caveats when comparing numbers.** Units differ: prompting iterations, steps and wall-clock time. JARVIS-1's "5x" holds only when its 60-minute result is set against VPT's 20-minute one; at equal time it is 6.22% vs 2.5%. ODYSSEY's "8/8" result is a zombie-combat task, not diamond. MindForge's 2.3x gain needed a GPT-4 expert coaching the open model. Mine AI MCP's documentation contradicts itself on whether block search covers all loaded chunks. Unless an agent states its x-ray, difficulty and day-cycle settings, assume its numbers are optimistic.

## 5. Small local models on 16 GB

- **Throughput.** Qwen2.5-14B Q4_K_M on a 16 GB RTX 3080 Laptop runs at about 28 tok/s generation and 858 tok/s prompt processing ([LocalScore](https://www.localscore.ai/model/2)). No 3080 Ti Laptop entry exists. Our estimate: a 3k-token prompt plus a 300-token plan takes about 15 s without thinking, and 1–2k thinking tokens add 40–70 s. Measure with `ollama run --verbose` when `model-status` reports IDLE.
- **Tool-calling quality.** qwen3:14B Q4_K_M scored F1 0.971 in Docker's 21-model test, close to GPT-4's 0.974, and quantization made no significant difference ([Docker](https://www.docker.com/blog/local-llm-tool-calling-a-practical-evaluation/)). Qwen3-14B scores 70.4 on BFCL with thinking and 61.5 without ([Qwen3](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.09388v1)).
- **Serving pitfalls.**
  - Qwen says greedy decoding in thinking mode causes endless repetition ([model card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-14B)). `docs/local-llm-integration.md` specifies temperature 0.
  - Ollama defaults to a 4k context on GPUs under 24 GiB ([docs](https://docs.ollama.com/context-length)).
  - llama.cpp does not inject the JSON schema into the prompt.
  - A closed Ollama bug ignored `format` when `think=false` ([#14645](https://github.com/ollama/ollama/issues/14645)). One project reported empty qwen3:14b outputs because thinking consumed the token budget _(anecdotal)_.
- **Router-sized models.** Qwen2.5-0.5B scores 27.9 on IFEval strict ([Qwen2.5](https://qwenlm.github.io/blog/qwen2.5-llm/)). A recent unreviewed preprint reports its tool-calling falling from 72% to 32% under sampling ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07370)). Fine-tuned tiny models do well on closed tool sets: TinyLlama went from 12.7% to 78.9% ([TinyAgent](https://bair.berkeley.edu/blog/2024/05/29/tiny-agent/)), and FunctionGemma 270M from 58% to 85% ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/functiongemma/)).
- **Distillation.** Few hundred to ~16k verified trajectories turn 7–8B models from near zero into usable agents. AgentEvol reached 64% on TextCraft from 300 trajectories ([AgentGym](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.04151)). MineCollab's SFT Llama-3-8B scored 0.28 on crafting, ahead of GPT-4o's 0.17; it was trained on 16k examples from about 2,000 trials, only around 200 of them successful ([MINDcraft](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.17950)).
- **Prompt format.** StateAct's Goal/State/Thought/Action format lifted Qwen2.5-14B on TextCraft from 31% to 37% ([StateAct](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.02810v3)). Put a short reasoning field before the plan steps: in JSON mode, GPT-3.5 always put its answer before its reasoning ([Speak Freely](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.02442)).

## 6. What to borrow for gtnh-agent (prioritized)

**P1. Quest-book goals and the verifier: implement the BQ client.**

- Add `BQ_NET_CHAN` to the outbound allowlist in `src/bot/gtnh1710/packets.ts`, along with an NBT writer and gzip slicing.
- Reply to `main_sync` at login. Parse `quest_sync`, `cache_sync` and `chapter_sync`. Send `task_checkbox`, `quest_action` 1 (submit), `choice_reward` and `quest_action` 0 (claim).
- Score "Finish Age 0" only from BQ's own completion records.
- Fix `quest-goals.ts`: include the 14 outside prerequisites and the smeltery XOR choice; honour taskLogic OR; treat crafting tasks as craft events while the quest is active.
- Gate quest-relevant crafting, kills and pickups on `activeQuests`, and re-submit after each unlock so items already held are counted.
- Claim rewards. They supply the iron pickaxe head ("Upgrade 2.0"), zinc dust, and the coins that buy zinc gravel ore.

**P2. Skill allowlist: build the Age 0 skills, invest here before planner size.**

- New skills: PLACE_BLOCK, USE_BLOCK, USE_ITEM, SELECT_STENCIL (the only mod payload, typed so it can only send registered stencils), CRAFT_PART, ASSEMBLE_TOOL, BUILD_MULTIBLOCK (coded blueprints for the smeltery and coke oven, verified block by block against S23), SMELTERY_LOAD, CAST_PART, RUN_COKE_OVEN and PROSPECT.
- Reuse the client's ModularUI VarInt encoder for item stacks inside mod payloads.
- Borrow pathing safety defaults from Mineflayer's pathfinder and Baritone: never break a block next to liquid, never mine under falling blocks, fall limit 3, parkour off. Add safe-to-cancel points and stuck escalation (AltoClef fails a move after less than 0.1 block of progress in 6 s, then wanders, then blacklists the target).

**P3. Keep "human-like" honest with a written fairness contract and a perception mask.**

- A block's identity enters the world model only if all of these hold: it has a face exposed to a non-opaque neighbour; an unobstructed ray from eye height reaches that face; and the face is lit (night sky light counts as 0 under darkerer). Alternatively it counts if it was the crosshair block within 4.5 blocks, or a block the bot broke. Seen blocks are remembered.
- Treat every other chunk-data block as unknown stone. That includes GT ore positions and their harvest-level metadata, which chunk data leaks even though GT hides the ore's material ([TileEntityOres](https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/GT5-Unofficial/blob/5.09.51.482/src/main/java/gregtech/common/blocks/TileEntityOres.java)).
- Hold GT packet 3 (ore material) for unseen positions until they are seen.
- Send VisualProspecting requests only for seen, crosshair-hit ores within reach, at least 2 s apart. The server answers anything within 32 blocks with no line-of-sight check, so it would work as an x-ray oracle ([ProspectingRequest](https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/VisualProspecting/blob/1.4.8/src/main/java/com/sinthoras/visualprospecting/network/ProspectingRequest.java)).
- Publish a settings card with every run: difficulty, day cycle, seed policy, what each skill abstracts, and no op commands. MineNPC-Task's bounded-knowledge policy is the template ([MineNPC](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.05215)).

**P4. Prospecting as a deterministic skill.** Read GT packet 19 at login to get the ore-grid pattern. New worlds use `floorMod(cx,3)=1 && floorMod(cz,3)=1`, not the wiki's `|x| mod 3` ([GTWorldgenerator](https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/GT5-Unofficial/blob/5.09.51.482/src/main/java/gregtech/common/GTWorldgenerator.java)). Sweep the surface by day. Treat small ores of vein-only materials as cues. Then dig a daylit 1x1 shaft at the ore-chunk center and stop at the first vein ore, since each ore chunk holds one vein. Our simulation estimates about 19 ore chunks and about 17 min in lowland terrain to find a copper vein and a cassiterite-sand vein. Tool tiers: a boosted flint pick mines copper ores and cassiterite sand; tin and cassiterite ore need level 3 ([Iguana materials](https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/IguanaTweaksTConstruct/blob/2.6.6/src/main/java/iguanaman/iguanatweakstconstruct/harvestlevels/TinkerMaterialTweaks.java)).

**P5. Recipe-tree goal decomposition from GTNH data.**

- Export recipes once from the same 2.8.4 build: NESQL or RecEx, plus hand-coded Tinkers' and GT tool recipes.
- Expand each active quest into raw-material totals and intermediate steps symbolically, in post-order (GITM-style), and give qwen3:14b a short checklist of valid options.
- Keep an XENON-style dependency graph that is revised from verified outcomes, and tell "wrong action" apart from "wrong recipe" before replanning.
- Validate every item name the planner emits against the registry.

**P6. System-1 router: deterministic, typed and pre-emptive.**

- Keep reflexes in code, bidding AltoClef-style priorities, and expose a safe-to-cancel flag on every executing skill.
- Tune them for this server: on Hard, 14 of every 17 creepers are Special Mobs variants. Death and Gravity creepers explode at power 5 ([SpecialMobs.cfg](https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/GT-New-Horizons-Modpack/blob/2.8.4/config/SpecialMobs.cfg)). 1 in 20 hostiles is an Infernal elite; ask the server over channel `AS_IF`, as the stock client does ([InfernalMobs.cfg](https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/GT-New-Horizons-Modpack/blob/2.8.4/config/InfernalMobs.cfg)). Healing stops below food 8 ([HungerOverhaul.cfg](https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/GT-New-Horizons-Modpack/blob/2.8.4/config/HungerOverhaul/HungerOverhaul.cfg)).
- Escalate to the planner on SwiftSage-style triggers ([SwiftSage](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2305.17390)): K steps without progress, an invalid action, or an unexpected observation. Also escalate on no quest progress for N minutes.
- Only consider a model router after fine-tuning on logged decisions. Even then, it outputs one enum label and sits behind the safety wrapper.

**P7. Planner configuration and latency.**

- Use explicit `think` and `num_ctx`, Qwen's recommended sampling (not temperature 0) with a fixed seed, the schema pasted into the prompt, and a reasoning field placed before `steps`.
- Plan asynchronously while the current validated step runs. Never call the planner during WAIT.
- Rebuild a compact state summary each call instead of accumulating a transcript. Cache re-reads were 87.7% of the Opus 5 run's cost.

**P8. Memory.**

- _Plan library._ Store verified allowlist sequences (macro-skills) as data, keyed by quest or sub-goal plus preconditions, and replay them before asking the planner. That keeps Voyager's skill-library benefit without letting the model write code.
- _World journal._ Keep a persistent record of seen blocks, prospected veins, containers and base layout outside the LLM context.
- _Distillation log._ Record every request, plan, per-step verification and outcome.

**P9. Loss and recovery.**

- Store items behind stone in more than one chest. Blast resistance: chest 12.5, bed 1, cobblestone 30.
- On death, OpenBlocks puts the inventory in a blast-proof grave within ±5 blocks of the death point ([PlayerDeathHandler](https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/OpenBlocks/blob/1.11.7-GTNH/src/main/java/openblocks/common/PlayerDeathHandler.java)). Plan during the 90 s of respawn invulnerability, then recover by day.
- Kill animals with a GT knife; other kills explode 10% of the time ([angermod.cfg](https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/GT-New-Horizons-Modpack/blob/2.8.4/config/GTNewHorizons/angermod.cfg)).
- Rotate foods, because Spice of Life cuts repeats.

**Pacing.** Expert speedrunners open their first coke oven in 9:23 ([Coke%](https://www.speedrun.com/gregtech_new_horizons/runs/yd415lvy)). An experienced, edited Let's Play reached coke ovens after about 2.9 h of video and bronze tools after about 4.8 h. Finishing all 92 quests in one 8-hour session is at or beyond expert pace. Budget several sessions, and report milestone curves: quests completed against wall-clock hours, deaths and tokens.
